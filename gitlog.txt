Author: JustinFan7777777 <justinfan7777777@gmail.com>
Date:   Fri May 29 19:31:38 2026 +0800

    fix: add x^0 term to LFSR feedback polynomial for max period
    
    The Fibonacci LFSR polynomial is x^16 + x^15 + x^14 + x^13 + x^4 + 1.
    The constant term (+1) requires including bit[0] of the shift register
    in the feedback XOR, not XORing a constant 1. This ensures the full
    65535-cycle period.
